How the score bands are doing

Pregame score vs what happened against the book's team total. Low bands (the under spots) should stay under the line more often than high bands. "Pts vs line" is actual points minus the posted line: negative means the under side was right on average.

Does the edge pay?

Edge is the book's team total minus the board's projection. Positive means the book has the team higher than we do, so the under is the play. At −110 you need 52.4% to break even. "Floor" is the 95% lower bound on the under rate given how many games are in the bucket; small buckets swing a lot.

Model and learning

How alerts reach you

  • Every run checks the data (board refreshed, games logged and graded, book lines and the injury report coming through). Problems open a GitHub issue on the repo, which emails you.
  • Accuracy alerts wait for 500 graded team-games with a posted line (about early December) so a bad week can't trigger one.
  • The weekly learner refits the weights every Monday. It changes the board only when new weights rank games against the book line better than the current ones on games they weren't fit on, at most once every 4 weeks and by at most 10 points per input. Every change opens an issue saying what changed and why.
  • Issues close themselves when the problem clears. Model-change issues stay open until you close them.
